The story

Light, mirrored around a single axis.

Each hood is only light, mirrored around a single axis. Where two touch, they burn a dark outline and a colour neither of them had. That is how new ones come to be.

The first seventy-one surfaced in three waves: the Founders. Their descendants carry their parents' features. The bloodlines branch, and sometimes grow back together: when two branches meet, a mask is born with two ancestors. That is why they carry the name GhostDAG, after the network where one block can have many parents.

In time, some descendants gave up colour and founded the Orders. Every meeting has its rite. Only rarely does the axis break. That is called the Schism.

1000 masks. 80 bloodlines. Numbered by lineage: each begins with its founder, or with the empty place a founder left.

Orders

Six orders gave up colour. The first never did.

Every mask belongs to one order. The Primal kept the first colours; Crossblood carry an order layered over primal colour.

How they were made

Grown in the GLSL Lab, chosen by eye.

Each mask is a raymarched fractal from the Pixel on Kaspa GLSL Lab, layered two or three times with a difference blend. Around 6,800 candidates were born across eleven generations. Each generation was bred from the masks kept in the one before, and every one of the thousand was chosen by eye.
